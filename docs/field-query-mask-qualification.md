## Primary-source finding

implements shape contact with a changing clip-depth latch. A mask hit resets the
latch; a mask miss replaces it with that mask's end depth. Ordinary children are
tested when their depth is at least the current latch. Static inspection therefore
predicts different outcomes from an all-active-masks conjunction for some
overlapping masks and exact end-depth controls. This is a source inference,
not an observation of the compiled browser runtime or Adobe Flash.

The field's two recovered authored ranges are nonoverlapping, with their ordinary
children strictly inside each range. Existing finite original controls do not
distinguish the competing policies at overlaps or exact boundaries. Runtime

clipDepth ranges; a complete query must not collapse those two mechanisms.
